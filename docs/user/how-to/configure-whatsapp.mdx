# How to configure WhatsApp

**Goal:** Customers message your business on WhatsApp and reach the same grounded Customer AI as your website widget.

## Prerequisites

- Plan that includes WhatsApp (typically **Growth+** — confirm on your billing page)
- Owner or Admin role
- Meta / WhatsApp Business assets ready (Business portfolio, WABA, phone number) — engineering or a partner may complete Meta-side setup
- Target **AI Workspace** with indexed knowledge

## Steps (operator)

1. Open [app.qefro.com](https://app.qefro.com) → **Channels** / WhatsApp settings (wording may vary slightly by release).
2. Select the **AI Workspace** that should answer WhatsApp traffic.
3. Complete provider connection (embedded signup or admin fields your org uses).
4. Confirm the **phone number** shows as connected / quality pending as applicable.
5. Send a test message from a personal WhatsApp to the business number.
6. In **Conversations**, open the thread and verify:
   - Reply is grounded (citations / correct policy)
   - Handoff behavior matches your instructions
7. Optional: enable Business Tools so WhatsApp users can check orders (same tools as widget).

<InfoBox>
WhatsApp template / marketing outbound rules are governed by Meta. Qefro focuses on the **AI session** once the customer is in a supported messaging context. Follow Meta policy for opt-in and templates.
</InfoBox>

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| No reply | Number bound? Workspace correct? API healthy? |
| Wrong knowledge | Different workspace than widget — align bindings |
| Tools fail | Same as web: tool auth / Customer Provider — ask engineering |
