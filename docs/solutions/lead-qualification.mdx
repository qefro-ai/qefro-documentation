# Lead qualification

**Outcome:** Website or WhatsApp visitors are guided through qualifying questions; good-fit leads are captured and pushed to your CRM or notify sales.

## Architecture (business view)

1. **Customer AI workspace** with product/pricing knowledge.
2. **Flow or structured prompts** collect company size, use case, timeline.
3. **Business Tool** creates/updates HubSpot (or other CRM) lead/contact.
4. Optional **approval or human handoff** for enterprise tier.
5. Optional **event** from marketing site form → same flow via [event-driven triggers](/docs/guides/event-driven-triggers).

## Prerequisites

- Workspace + knowledge for accurate product answers
- CRM tool (HubSpot integration or custom SDK tool) — [HubSpot](/docs/integrations/hubspot/overview)
- Clear definition of qualified vs disqualified
- Channel: widget and/or WhatsApp

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Leads missing in CRM | Tool logs; field mapping; auth |
| Low quality leads | Tighten questions; add disqualify paths |
| Spam | Rate limits, challenge, or human review step |
