# What is Qefro?

Qefro is an **AI Workspace platform** for organizations. You configure knowledge, Business Tools, and Business Flows in one place, then deploy:

- **Customer AI** — website widget and WhatsApp, grounded in your knowledge with citations
- **Employee AI** — branded Internal Portal for internal teams with RBAC

Assistants do more than answer questions: they can run **Business Actions** (secure tool calls into your systems) and **Business Flows** (orchestrated multi-step processes with approvals, delays, and challenges).

<InfoBox>
**Configure once. Deploy everywhere.** Workspaces isolate knowledge and tools per team (Support, HR, IT). Events, chat, and schedules all enter the same Runtime.
</InfoBox>
